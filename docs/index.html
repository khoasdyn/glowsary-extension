How it works

Three small moments, every day

Save, glow, hover — repeated until the words are yours.

Step 1

Save it as you read

Select a word, right-click, and choose "Add word". Write the meaning in your own words, add aliases, and pick a color.

Step 2

See it glow everywhere

From now on, that word gets a subtle underline on every site you visit. You recognize it on sight, without doing anything.

Step 3

Hover to remember

Your definition appears in a small popup next to the word, then gets out of your way. Your reading is never interrupted.

The management view

One cozy home for every word

Everything you saved, in a colorful grid you can search, sort, and shape.

Flip the tabs, just like in the app

The Home tab keeps your word collection; the Settings tab keeps the quiet machinery. Click the tabs above to peek at both.

  • Search any word instantly, and sort by latest added or A to Z.
  • Hear any saved word spoken aloud from its card.
  • Exclude sites where you want to read with no highlights.
  • Export and import your whole list as a CSV backup.
Features

Small features that make words stick

Four colors

Tag each saved word with a color to group them and tell them apart at a glance.

Aliases

Let "version" and "versions" share one definition and one highlight, so every form of the word glows.

Many meanings

The same word can hold several saved definitions. The popup pages through them, newest first.

Hear it spoken

A speaker icon pronounces any saved word out loud, so you learn the sound, not only the meaning.

You stay in control

One switch turns all highlighting off, and you can exclude any site where you want to read normally.

Backup in your hands

Export your whole list as a CSV file anytime, and import it back on any computer.

Privacy

Private by design.

Glowsary has no servers to send your words to. Everything lives in your browser, on your own computer, and nowhere else.

  • No account, no sign-in. Install it and start saving words right away.
  • No tracking. Glowsary never watches what you read or phones home.
  • Your words stay local. The only copy is yours — export it as a CSV whenever you like.
