Glowsary can auto-generate word definitions for you using AI. To turn this on, you add your own free Gemini API key — it stays on your device and runs on your own Google account. This short video shows you exactly how.

Get your free Gemini API key here: [https://aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com/)

Steps in this video:

1. Open Glowsary and go to the Settings tab
2. Create a free key at [https://aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com/)
3. Paste the key into the API Key field and click Save
4. Add a word and tap auto-generate to fill the definition for you

Note: Only Gemini is supported for now. Your key is stored on your own device, and the definitions are generated through your own Google account.
