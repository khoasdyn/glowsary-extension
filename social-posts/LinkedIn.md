I just shipped a new version of Glowsary, a small side project I keep building on.

It's a Chrome extension that helps you remember vocabulary while you read in another language online.

The reason I started it is simple.

I read a lot of English articles on Medium and Substack, and I kept looking up the same word again and again without ever remembering it.

Every new page felt like the first time I'd met that word.

With Glowsary, you save a word with your own meaning, or let AI draft one for you in a tap.

After that, the word gets a subtle underline on every site you read, and the meaning only appears when you hover over it.

It works for any language you are learning, not just English.

You can also add an image to each word, and everything runs fully on your device, no account needed.

This new version is live on the Chrome Web Store now, and I'm still adding more.
