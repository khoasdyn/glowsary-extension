```
I kept looking up the same English words while reading online, then forgetting them.

So I built Glowsary: a Chrome extension that saves a word with your own meaning, then quietly underlines it everywhere you read. Hover to see your note.

Free, private, on your device.
```

```
Now live on the Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/glowsary-build-your-vocab/bfnlhjdjkblhenfhnmdoghfomekcekmj

Other things it does: AI-drafted definitions, word pronunciation, images per word, color tags, and a per-site off switch.
```
