# Glowsary Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Glowsary is a browser extension that lets you save words and phrases with your own definitions while you read on the web, highlights those saved words on the pages you visit, and shows your saved definition in a small popup when you hover or click.

## The short version

Glowsary does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you save stays on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.

## What Glowsary stores

Glowsary stores the following on your own device, using your browser's local storage:

- The words and phrases you save, your definitions, and any aliases you add.
- Your settings, such as whether highlighting is on or off and whether the definition shows on hover or click.
- Your list of excluded sites, where you have chosen to turn highlighting off.

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, because Glowsary has no server and makes no network connection to send your data.

## Page content

To highlight your saved words, Glowsary reads the text of the pages you visit inside your browser to find matches. This reading happens entirely on your device. Glowsary does not record, store, or send the content of the pages you read.

## Why Glowsary asks for permissions

- Reading and changing data on the websites you visit: needed to find and underline your saved words on the pages you read and to show their definitions.
- Storage: needed to save your words, settings, and excluded sites on your device.
- Tabs: needed to apply your setting changes across open tabs and to avoid opening duplicate settings tabs. It is not used to track your browsing history.
- Right-click menu: needed to add the "Add word" action so you can save selected text.

## Export and import

Glowsary lets you export your saved words to a file and import them back. This is started by you, and the file is saved to your own computer. Glowsary does not upload these files anywhere.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any saved word from the management view at any time. Removing the extension from your browser deletes all of the data Glowsary stored on your device.

## Children

Glowsary is a general-purpose reading tool and is not directed at children.
